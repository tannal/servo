1. <mrow> Baseline vs. Math Axis Alignment (Figure 10 & 11)

"The child boxes are put in a row one after the other with all their alphabetic baselines aligned... fraction bars or symmetric stretchy operators will also be aligned along the math axis."
x + a+1 b = y − 5
Observation Layout Invariant
Variables ($x, y$) & digits sit exactly on the solid red line. Alphabetic Baseline Alignment across all child boxes in the <mrow>.
The fraction bar, $+$, $-$, and $=$ centers lie on the dashed orange line. Math Axis Alignment: Centered at distance AxisHeight above the baseline.

2. <mfrac> Non-Zero vs. Zero Linethickness & DisplayStyle (Figure 12 & 13)

"NumeratorShift & DenominatorShift ensure the fraction bar is at AxisHeight... If linethickness is zero, StackTopShiftUp and StackBottomShiftDown are used."
0 + 1 2 − 1 2 + 1 234 − ( n k )
Fraction Variant Layout Behavior
displaystyle="true" Uses FractionNumDisplayStyleGapMin; numerator & denominator maintain larger script font size.
Compact (Default) Uses compact shift values; child elements automatically receive math-depth: auto-add; math-style: compact;
linethickness="4px" Rule block size expands; gaps are measured from the edge of the thicker bar.
linethickness="0" (Binomial) Fraction bar omitted entirely; StackGapMin ensures numbers do not collide. Fences stretch around it.

3. Multi-Pass Symmetric Stretchy Operators (Figure 11)

"LToStretch operators stretch symmetrically around the Math Axis: Target Ascent = AxisHeight + HalfHeight, Target Descent = HalfHeight - AxisHeight."
( 1 + 2 3 4 ) = [ x y ]

Notice how the left and right parentheses ( ) stretch tall to enclose the multi-level nested fraction, but remain centered on the Math Axis, not skewed towards the larger numerator.

4. Scripts: <msub>, <msup>, <msubsup> & Italic Correction

"The msub base's alphabetic baseline matches the container baseline. Subscript is shifted down by SubShift; Superscript is shifted up by SuperShift."
X 1 + X 2 + X 1 2 + ∫ 0 ∞ f ( x ) d x
Script Component Alignment & Kerning Invariant
<msubsup> Gap SubSuperGapMin prevents the subscript and superscript from colliding vertically.
Italic Correction On slanted symbols like $\int$, the superscript is shifted horizontally to the right by ItalicCorrection to clear the top overhang.

5. <mpadded> Arbitrary Metric Overrides (Figure 16)

"The mpadded element generates an anonymous <mrow> box with size and baseline modified according to width, height, depth, lspace, and voffset."
1 + a b + a b + 9

The amber <mpadded> box has explicitly overridden width, expanded height/depth, shifted the fraction horizontally via lspace, and dropped the baseline using negative voffset.

6. Tabular Math <mtable> (CSS Table Integration)

"The mtable element is laid out as an inline-table. The center of the table is aligned with the math axis."

Notice that the entire 3x3 identity matrix's vertical center aligns with the Math Axis (matching the fraction bar and the equals sign).
